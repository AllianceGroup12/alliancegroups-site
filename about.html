Corporate Overview

Alliance Group is an established Queensland contracting firm specializing in commercial building construction, industrial metal roofing, and Class A & B hazardous material remediation. We partner with commercial asset managers, insurance companies, and government entities statewide.

Safety is fundamental to all our operations. Holding WorkSafe QLD Class A friable asbestos certification, our protocols align strictly with ISO 45001 safety management systems and EPA regulated waste tracking standards.

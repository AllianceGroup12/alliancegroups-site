About Alliance Group Queensland

Over 15 years delivering commercial building construction, industrial roofing, and licensed hazardous material removal with an absolute commitment to safety and regulatory compliance.

Corporate Overview

Alliance Group is an established Queensland contracting firm specializing in commercial building construction, industrial metal roofing, and Class A & B hazardous material remediation. We partner with commercial asset managers, insurance companies, and government entities statewide.

Safety & Compliance Commitment

Safety is fundamental to all our operations. Holding QBCC Open Builder licensing and WorkSafe QLD Class A friable asbestos certification, our protocols align strictly with ISO 45001 safety management systems and EPA regulated waste tracking standards.

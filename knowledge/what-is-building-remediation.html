What is Building Remediation?

An engineering guide to structural building remediation, concrete spalling repairs, waterproofing renewals, and asset preservation across Queensland.

Building Remediation Services Strata Case Study

Remediation vs. General Renovation

  • ✓ Engineering Distinction: Renovation focuses on aesthetic and cosmetic updates. Building remediation is the structural and engineered rectification of underlying defects, water ingress, or material degradation to restore structural capacity and legal code compliance.
  • ✓ Asset Value Preservation: Proactive remediation protects property capital value and prevents catastrophic structural failure.

Core Disciplines of Building Remediation

  1. Concrete Spalling Rectification ("Concrete Cancer"): Breaking out carbonated concrete, treating oxidized steel rebar with zinc-rich coatings, installing sacrificial zinc anodes, and reinstating with polymer-modified structural mortar.
  2. Podium & Balcony Waterproofing Renewal: Complete removal of failed tiles and screeds, application of Class III polyurethane or polyurea membranes, and re-screeding to compliant falls (1:100).
  3. Masonry & Foundation Stabilization: Helical stainless steel stitching of cracked cavity brickwork, lintel replacement, and underpinning coordination.
  4. Facade Refurbishment & Joint Resealing: Replacement of failed polyurethane expansion joints, concrete sealing, and carbonation barrier coatings.

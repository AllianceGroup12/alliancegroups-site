What to Do if Asbestos is Discovered

An immediate step-by-step emergency protocol for builders, trades, and homeowners if suspected asbestos is uncovered or accidentally disturbed during renovation.

Book Urgent Sample Testing ($165) 📞 Call Hazmat Response: 0403 126 276

Immediate Stop-Work Mandate

  • ✗ DO NOT VACUUM OR SWEEP: Standard domestic or shop vacuums blow microscopic asbestos fibers directly through their filters into the room air, creating a severe airborne health hazard.
  • ✓ STOP WORK IMMEDIATELY: Cease all power tools, sawing, or demolition in the area and isolate the room.

The 5-Step Stop-Work Action Protocol

  1. Step 1: Cease Work & Evacuate the Zone: Immediately power down all tools. Have everyone vacate the immediate work area to prevent tracking debris.
  2. Step 2: Isolate Air Circulation & Seal Doors: Turn off air conditioning units, fans, and ventilation systems. Close doors and windows to contain airborne dust.
  3. Step 3: Post Warning Signage: Barricade the entry with hazard tape or a clear handwritten notice: "DANGER – SUSPECTED ASBESTOS – DO NOT ENTER".
  4. Step 4: Engage Licensed NATA Testing: Have a licensed hazmat technician take a controlled micro-sample for 24-hour Polarized Light Microscopy (PLM) analysis.
  5. Step 5: Licensed Removal & Clearance: If positive, engage a Class A or Class B licensed contractor to remove the material under full containment and issue a Clearance Certificate.
⚖ WorkSafe Queensland Legal Obligation

Under the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (QLD), any workplace where uncontrolled asbestos disturbance occurs must implement immediate containment measures and prevent re-entry until certified safe.

What is a Building Defect Inspection?

A guide to forensic diagnostic methodology, non-destructive testing tools, structural causation analysis, and actionable rectification reporting in Queensland.

Book Defect Inspection Audit Building Remediation

Defect Inspection Definition

  • ✓ Beyond Standard Pre-Purchase Checklists: A forensic defect inspection is an in-depth, engineering-grade investigation designed to establish the root physical cause of structural movement, waterproofing breakdown, or material deterioration.
  • ✓ Tender-Ready Rectification Scopes: The output is not just a list of problems, but a detailed technical scope specifying repair methods and Australian Standards compliance.

When is a Forensic Defect Inspection Warranted?

  • Persistent Water Ingress: Water leaking into habitable spaces despite repeated contractor patching.
  • Concrete Cracking & Spalling: Rust bleeding, cracking lintels, or chunks of concrete detaching from balconies.
  • Body Corporate Warranty Claims: Preparing statutory defect lists within the statutory 6-year structural defect warranty period.
  • Insurance Dispute Resolution: Establishing whether damage is attributable to storm event impact versus historical maintenance wear.

Tools & Non-Destructive Testing Methods Used

  • Infrared Thermography: FLIR high-resolution thermal imaging to identify hidden thermal bridges, moisture pooling, and insulation voids.
  • Electronic Moisture Impedance Testing: Tramex non-invasive scanners to map moisture saturation depths across masonry, concrete, and timber.
  • Diagnostic Flood & Spray Testing: Calibrated water pressure testing to recreate and isolate leak pathways through windows, flashings, and membranes.
  • Drone Aerial Surveying: 4K visual and thermal aerial capture of multi-storey facades, parapets, and inaccessible roof areas.

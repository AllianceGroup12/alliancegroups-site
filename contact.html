Licensing & Compliance FAQs

Are you licensed for both Class A and Class B asbestos removal?

Yes. Alliance Group holds Class A (friable asbestos) and Class B (bonded asbestos) licenses issued by WorkSafe Queensland, allowing us to safely manage any asbestos contamination.

Do you assist with insurance claims?

Yes, we regularly work directly with major Australian insurance assessors to provide rapid storm damage reports, make-safe tarping, and full scope reconstruction.

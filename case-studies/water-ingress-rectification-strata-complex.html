Strata Water Ingress Rectification

How Alliance Group diagnosed and permanently cured chronic 3-year water ingress across a 32-unit residential strata complex in South Brisbane.

Water Ingress Diagnostics Building Remediation Scope

Project Summary & Key Metrics

  • ✓ Scope: Forensic electronic leak detection, removal of 380m² failed podium tiles, concrete crack injection, installation of Class III dual-layer polyurea membrane, and continuous threshold drain installation.
  • ✓ Outcome: 100% dry basement verified across subsequent severe wet seasons, concrete spalling halted, body corporate insurance excess reduced from $25,000 to $1,000.

The Client Problem

A 4-storey residential apartment complex in South Brisbane experienced chronic water leaking into the basement carpark directly beneath the landscaped common podium deck. Over three years, the Body Corporate spent in excess of $45,000 on ad-hoc patch contractors who injected polyurethane foam into ceiling cracks without identifying the surface ingress point.

The water continued to pool behind electrical sub-boards and caused extensive concrete cancer spalling along load-bearing beams.

The Alliance Diagnostic Investigation

  1. Thermal Imaging & Electronic Moisture Mapping: Scanned the underside of the slab and podium deck, revealing that water was travelling 18 metres horizontally beneath the screed before exiting into the carpark.
  2. Diagnostic Flood Isolation Testing: Conducted isolated zone flood testing across planters, expansion joints, and balcony thresholds. The test revealed that the original liquid membrane had torn along the structural expansion joint due to building movement.

The Engineered Remediation Execution

  • Screed & Tile Demolition: Stripped the 380m² failed tiles and saturated bedding screed back to the structural concrete slab.
  • Substrate Preparation & Crack Stitching: Ground the slab surface, injected structural epoxy into all active substrate cracks, and installed heavy-duty elastomeric joint tape.
  • High-Elongation Membrane Application: Applied a continuous, spray-applied polyurea waterproof membrane with 450% elongation capacity to accommodate future structural movement.
  • Re-screeding & Compliance Handover: Laid new screeds with 1:80 falls to storm drainage pits and installed commercial anti-slip porcelain tiles.
